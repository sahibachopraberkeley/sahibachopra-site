About

I study how new technologies change work and organizations, especially as they move from exciting tools to part of how work actually gets done. My current focus is generative AI: how it changes productivity, workflows, evaluation, expertise, and organizational design.

Technology adoption is never just a technical problem. Identical tools produce different outcomes depending on implementation, users, context, and how organizations evaluate work.

  1. How is AI changing collaboration and coordination at work?
  2. How do organizations build the infrastructure to use AI at scale?
  3. What should be transparent when AI is involved in work?
  4. Who benefits from new technologies, and who gets left out?
